# Privacy Policy – Tambola Number Announcer

**Developer:** Oneway Developers (Siddhant Agarwal)
**Last updated:** 15 November 2026

Tambola Number Announcer ("the app") is a Housie / Tambola number caller. This policy explains what information is used when you use the app.

## Information the app itself collects
The app does **not** ask you to create an account, and it does **not** collect your name, phone number, contacts, photos or location. Game data (called numbers and your settings) stays on your device only.

## Text-to-Speech
Numbers are spoken using your phone's built-in Text-to-Speech engine. The app does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
The app shows ads provided by **Google AdMob**. To show ads, Google may collect and process:
- Your device's advertising ID
- Approximate location (based on IP address)
- Device information (model, OS version) and app usage / diagnostic data such as ad interactions and crash information

Google uses this to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area and the UK, you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice any time via **Privacy settings** in the app.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Learn more about how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
